# Architecture decision records

ADRs capture decisions that materially constrain implementation or maintenance.
Their status is one of `Proposed`, `Accepted`, `Superseded`, or `Rejected`.

Phase 0 experiments may continue while ADRs are proposed. Phase 1 must not begin
until ADR-0001 and ADR-0002 have been reviewed and accepted by the maintainer.

| ADR | Title | Status |
|---|---|---|
| [0001](0001-independent-implementation.md) | Independent implementation and licensing | Proposed |
| [0002](0002-data-access.md) | Elevation and imagery data access | Proposed |
